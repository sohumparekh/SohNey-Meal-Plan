# How we use it

One page for us. Everything is in the **Menu** app on the Home Screen.

## Every week (about 5 minutes)

**Thursday night** a notification says next week's menu is ready.
1. Open it. The **Week** tab shows all 21 meals (Saturday to Friday).
2. Tap any meal you don't want. Pick from the 5 suggestions (best fits are on top, and
   they say if they need anything extra).
3. Tap **Approve week**. The shopping lists are made.

**Friday at the office**
1. **Shopping, Shubham tab.** Tap **Call Shubham** and read out the list, or walk over
   and tap **Show at counter**.
2. Pick it up in the evening. At home, tick what came. Tap **Pickup done**: anything
   unticked moves to the top-up list, and the app warns which meals need it.
3. **Blinkit tab.** Order these in one go. **Find** opens a Blinkit search.
4. **Staples tab.** Only what's due (atta every 3 weeks, and so on) or marked "running low".

**Tuesday** (only if the app says so): a top-up notification lists a few fresh items
for Wednesday to Friday. Pick them up from Shubham on the way back from office.

## Every day (one tap)

**9 pm** a notification says *Send tomorrow's menu to Vimal*. Tap it, check it, tap the
green button. WhatsApp opens with the message ready. Tap Send. Done.

The message already has all three meals, a Plan B for each, and anything she must soak
or prepare tonight.

## When plans change

- **Change a meal:** Today or Week tab, **Swap**. Works from either of our phones.
- **Eating out:** tap **Eating out** on the meal. The app moves that dish's fresh items
  to a later day if it can, and gives you a **Tell the cook** button.
- **Changed tonight's dinner after the 9 pm message?** At 4 pm the app reminds you to
  send her the change, only if something actually changed.
- **Guests:** use **+ / −** on the meal. Quantities on the lists scale.
- **Vimal says something ran out:** paste or type her message in **Vimal replied?** on
  the Today tab (for example "paneer khatam"). The item goes on the top-up list, and any
  meal in the next 2 days that needs it switches to Plan B.
- **Pantry running low** (atta, ghee, jeera...): Shopping, Staples, search, **Mark low**.

## Adding a dish

**Dishes**, then **+ Add dish**. Paste an Instagram or YouTube link, or just type the name.
Check the ingredients, tap **Save**. It joins the suggestions straight away. The first
time it's on the menu, the message to Vimal includes a short recipe.

## After a meal

Tap 👍 or 👎 on Today. Dishes we don't like come up less often.
