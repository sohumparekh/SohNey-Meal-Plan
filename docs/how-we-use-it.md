# How I use it

Everything is in the **Ghar ka Menu** app. It works without internet.

## Every day: 2 taps

**9 pm** a notification: *Send tomorrow's menu to Vimal*. Tap it, then tap the green
**Send to Vimal**. WhatsApp opens in her chat with the message ready. Tap Send.

It has all three meals, a Plan B for each, and anything she must soak or prepare tonight.

**4 pm** a notification: *Check tonight's dinner*. If nothing changed, ignore it. If it
did, tap it and send.

## Every week: about 5 minutes

**Thursday 8 pm**, *Plan next week*: tap it. All 21 meals (Saturday to Friday) are on one
screen. Tap any meal to swap it (best fits come first), then tap **Approve week**.

**Friday 10 am**, *Send grocery order*: tap it, then **Send order**. The Shubham list
opens in WhatsApp. (Or use **Call** / **Show at counter** on the Shopping tab.)

At home, tick what came, then **Pickup done**: anything not ticked moves to the Top-up
list. Order the **Blinkit** tab in one go. The **Staples** tab only shows what's due.

## When plans change

- **Swap a meal:** Today or Week tab → Swap.
- **Eating out:** tap **Eating out** on the meal. Its fresh items move to a later day if
  possible, and you get a **Tell the cook** button.
- **Guests:** + / − on the meal; the lists scale.
- **Vimal says something ran out:** type her message in **Vimal replied?** on Today
  ("paneer khatam"). It goes on the top-up list and that meal switches to Plan B.
- **Pantry low:** Shopping → Staples → search → **Mark low**.

## New dish

Dishes → **+ Add dish** → **Add with Claude**: type the dish or paste a link, tap **Copy
prompt**, paste it in the Claude app, copy Claude's reply, come back, **Paste from
clipboard**, check, **Save**. Or choose **Type it in**.

## Data

Stored only on this phone, backed up every week to `Documents/GharKaMenu`. **Share plan**
sends everything as one file (for a new phone, or later for Neyomi's phone); **Import**
loads it.
